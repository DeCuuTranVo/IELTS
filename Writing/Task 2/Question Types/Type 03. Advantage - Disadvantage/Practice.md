To sum up, while there are some advantages of supporting students to get part-time jobs while studying at school, it is undeniable that part-time jobs occupy students' time and consume their energy, thus having significant negative impacts on their academic performance.

## Question:

### Essay

However, I believe that the advantages of such autonomous systems significantly outweigh their disadvantages, primarily because of the enhanced safety features that computerized systems offer.

On the one hand, self-driving cars impose certain risks of data breaches to people who are inside the car and on the road. One drawback is data breaches, which comes from the fact that the car constantly collects data about its locations, directions, and destinations, then transmits them to remote servers for processing purposes. If hackers successfully infiltrate the system, they will know the where the car is and traveling patterns of the car owner. As a result, the people sitting in the vehicle may be put at the danger of car thief, robbery, or even kidnapping. Therefore, the use of autonomous driving system jeopardize humans to a certain extent regarding personal data infringement. 

On the other hand, embracing self-driving vehicles in our daily lives greatly reduces human errors in traffic. More specifically, computerized driving systems inherently do not have several weaknesses like human beings, such as fatigue and emotional influence. For example, people can feel tired and drowsy when driving at night, causing them to miss out the turning signals of the car ahead and bump into it. In some cases, when unexpected events occur, notice drivers are frozen from fear and forget to hit the brakes, which worsens the damage of the collision. In contrast, autonomous vehicles continuously monitor road conditions and adjust driving speeds accordingly to keep safe distances from surrounding vehicles in a tireless and emotionless manner. Thus, this advanced technology greatly reduces accident rates and protects car drivers better.

To sum up, though the application of self-driving automobiles threatens the personal privacy and security of car drivers, I believe that allowing autonomous driving vehicles to participate in traffic is clearly the right thing to do, since this protect people from traffic accidents, thereby saving their lives.
