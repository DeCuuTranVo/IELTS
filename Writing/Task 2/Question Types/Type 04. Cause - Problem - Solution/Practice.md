The main cause of relics being destroyed is the conflict between public interests and personal interests of tourists. First and foremost, many people want to record their presences in a place that they have never been at before. Unfortunately, a considerable number of visitors lacking awareness fulfill this wish by writing and carving their names and signatures on building walls. These irresponsible acts result in unsightly building surfaces, reducing the overall aesthetics of the affected architectural structures. Furthermore, there is a need for souvenirs as a means to keep memories of their trips. While tourists can simply take memorable photographs and bring them home, many of them take home historical artifacts instead. For example, many relics have to go through annual restoration since stone debris are regularly taken away from walls and houses by their visitors. As a result, impacted historic constructions become fragile and prone to the weather.

In fact, the number of recorded cases of vandalism in historical sites in my city decreased significantly after strict conservation policies are enforced and the content of those policies is broadcast to the public.

## Question: 
Many women are in the workforce today, but only a few are in senior positions. 
What cause this phenomenon?
Suggest possible solutions that would resolve this issue.
### Essay
It is true that women are underrepresented in senior-level roles these days. While this is a serious problem, it can be solved by letting men help women with their family concerns and permitting women to work on flexible schedules and locations.

The main cause of the issue is that women have to choose between their careers and their families. Social beliefs often place the burden of housework and child care on married women. Given the stable financial support from the breadwinners, once the homemakers devote most of their time and energy to their families, they have less motivation to advance their careers. For example, many friends of mine decide to become full-time housewives after getting married so that they can enjoy the lifestyle of a traditional wife. Furthermore, taking care of children, especially infants, consumes a lot of time during the day. This makes women having small children find that nine-to-five work in the office are too demanding for them regarding fixed schedules and high expectations of productiveness. In fact, a major number of women have children, burn out, and quit their jobs before reaching the seniority required for high-level positions.

However, there are solutions to this problem. One of the ways to improve the situation is to let husbands share household consideration with their wives. By organizing education campaigns on media about the shared duties towards their marriage, the government can persuade husbands to take on a greater share of housework and childcare, enabling their wives to spend time pursuing their own careers. For instance, I have seen a lot of successful female professors who have caring partners actively babysitting their children so that they can focus on their doctoral theses. In addition, companies should introduce new policies facilitating remote work and flexible working hours to help female employees concurrently fulfill the roles of a mother, a wife, and a worker. In actuality, a significantly higher percentage of female managers are observed in big corporates because such favorable policies allow their female staff to stay in their roles and be productive long enough to reach senior positions.

To sum up, it is clear that few females are in top-level positions in the contemporary workforce. Nevertheless, the issue can be resolved by letting women share their traditional duties with their husbands and benefits from flexible work arrangements and work-from-home policies.
